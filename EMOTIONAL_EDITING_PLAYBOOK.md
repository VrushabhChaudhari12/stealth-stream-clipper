# MASTER EDITING EXECUTION RULES (FOR EDITORS & AI PIPELINES)

### Rule 1: The 400ms Audio Decibel Drop
Whenever an emotional reversal occurs (the realization of a leak, the betrayal of a donation, the discovery of a clean gamer), **NEVER** let background music keep playing. A failure to cut music destroys comedic timing. Cut audio volume to `-inf dB` for **between 300ms and 500ms**. Human comedic timing lives in that vacuum.

### Rule 2: The Two-Tier Kinetic Subtitle Rule
* Keep active subtitles to **1 to 3 words maximum per screen frame**. Never display long, multi-line blocks of text.
* The spoken word must highlight in the archetype's designated color **at the exact phoneme onset** (0-millisecond latency).
* On screaming or high emotional spikes, scale text size by +30% with a 2-frame overshoot bounce.

### Rule 3: Visual Split Ratio Physics (Vertical 9:16)
* For gameplay + facecam videos, allocate **45% top for facecam** and **55% bottom for gameplay/screen action**.
* When the emotional focus is 100% on a reaction (e.g., Speed's scream or Ron's shock), **cut to full-screen 9:16 facecam** for 1.2 to 2.5 seconds, then cut back to the split.

### Rule 4: Soundboard Balance & Dynamic Range
* Dialogue Speech Target: `-12dB to -8dB` RMS.
* Background Music (Ducked): `-24dB to -20dB`.
* Comedic SFX (Vine Boom, Shutter, Bonk): `-6dB to -4dB` Peak (must punch through the mix without clipping master output).
* Ear-rape / Screaming moments: Soft limiter ceiling at `-1.0dB` with mild tube saturation to avoid harsh digital square-wave distortion.

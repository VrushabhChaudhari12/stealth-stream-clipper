# 🚀 YouTube Shorts Viral Engine — Sovereign Rules & 2026 Algorithm Blueprint

This document codifies the operational rules, algorithmic retention triggers, and production standards for autonomous YouTube Shorts clipping.

---

## 1. CLIP SELECTION ARCHITECTURE
- **Target Length**: 25–45 seconds. **Hard max 60s**. Sweet spot: 30–35s.
- **Trigger Moments**: Emotional peaks, opinion bombs, revelations, conflict, quotable lines, story peaks, and practical value.
- **Scoring Threshold**: Score each candidate 1–10 on:
  - **Hook (0–3s)**
  - **Energy**
  - **Pacing**
  - **Payoff clarity**
  - **Re-watchability**
  *Only publish clips scoring ≥ 7/10.*
- **Stream Ratio**: From a 1-hour stream, extract 3–5 clips maximum. *Quality > Quantity*.
- **Strict Exclusions**: No dead air, no "um"s, no pauses > 0.5s, no boring transitions.
- **Pacing**: Punch in (1.14x zoom) on vocal emphasis.

---

## 2. THE HOOK (First 1.5 Seconds — Decides 90% of Performance)
- **Zero Intro Friction**: NO channel logos, NO "hey guys", NO "in today's video", NO delayed setup.
- **Frame 1**: Face mid-emotion, contradictory claim on screen, or counter-intuitive visual.
- **Spoken Rule**: First spoken sentence must be **< 10 words** and promise a specific payoff.
- **Hook Format Rotation**:
  1. **Numbered Curiosity**: *"3 mistakes I made before..."*
  2. **Specific Outcome**: *"This 1 setting doubled my FPS"*
  3. **Pattern Interrupt**: *[Shocking Visual]* + *"You've been doing this wrong"*
  4. **Controversial Claim**: *"Why GTA 6 is actually worse than GT6 and here's why"*

---

## 3. THE 4-BEAT STRUCTURAL SPINE
| Beat | Timestamp | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **1. HOOK** | 0–3s | Open with tension or immediate outcome — never setup |
| **2. BUILD** | 3–15s | Essential context only; escalate stakes around second 8 |
| **3. PAYOFF** | 15–45s | Deliver the promised answer or reaction. One clear idea only |
| **4. LOOP** | Last 2s | Ending flows seamlessly back into Frame 1 (triggers re-watches > 100% retention) |

---

## 4. VIRAL EDITING & POST-PRODUCTION RULES
- **Visual Rhythm**: Change something visually every **3–5 seconds** (micro-zoom, angle swap, text emphasis).
- **Subtitles**:
  - 2–4 words per burst.
  - ALL CAPS, bold font (Impact / Arial Bold / Montserrat Black).
  - Real-time word karaoke highlighting (Yellow `#00FFFF` active word, White `#FFFFFF` inactive words).
  - Placed in bottom third — **never** covering the creator's face.
- **Color Grading**: Measurably beats raw source: +4% contrast, +12% saturation, subtle unsharp sharpening.
- **Audio Ducking**:
  - GTA background music ducked **-12dB to -18dB** whenever streamer speaks.
  - No licensed pop tracks (use copyright-free / ambient stems).
- **Ending**: Clean, hard cut immediately after the payoff. **Zero outro. Never say 'subscribe'.**
- **Framing**: True 9:16 vertical (1080x1920, 60fps). 16:9 is not a Short.

---

## 5. THUMBNAIL ARCHITECTURE
- **Layout**: Single high-emotion face or peak action + **3–4 words max of text**.
- **Non-Duplication Rule**: Thumbnail text must **NOT** repeat the video title. They form a complimentary pairing.
  - *Title*: "Speed's 1 Mistake That Broke GTA 6 Forever"
  - *Thumbnail Text*: "NO WAY BROKE IT! 😱"
- **Visuals**: Saturated, high contrast, readable at small phone notification size.

---

## 6. TITLE SPECIFICATIONS
- **Length**: **40–60 characters** (mobile UI truncates beyond 60).
- **Formula**: `[Specific Claim] + [Emotional Trigger]`
- **Mandatory**: Include one specific number (e.g. `1`, `3`, `$10,000`, `0.001s`) or proper noun.
- **Format Examples**:
  - *"Speed's 1 Mistake That Broke GTA 6 Forever"* (41 chars)
  - *"This 1 Hidden Setting Doubled Speed's FPS"* (40 chars)
  - *"Speed Leaked 3 Secret GTA 6 Codes On Stream"* (43 chars)
  - *"The $10,000 GTA 6 Key Speed Gave Away"* (38 chars)

---

## 7. DESCRIPTION & HASHTAGS (4-Tier System)
YouTube flags excessive hashtags as spam. **Use 3–5 hashtags total.**

- **Tier 1 (Broad)**: `#shorts` `#viral` `#fyp`
- **Tier 2 (Niche)**: `#gta6` `#ispeed` `#streamer`
- **Tier 3 (Category)**: `#gaming` `#ps5` `#live`
- **Tier 4 (Emotional)**: `#insane` `#unbelievable` `#waitforit`

**Description Format**:
```
[1-2 sentence hook explaining the moment]. Watch the full stream: [link]

#shorts #gaming #gta6 #viral
```

---

## 8. POSTING SCHEDULE & CADENCE (India / IST)
- **Best Windows**:
  - Morning Window: **8:00 AM – 11:00 AM IST**
  - Evening Window: **6:00 PM – 9:00 PM IST**
- **Volume**: 1–2 Shorts per day max. Consistency > volume.
- **Dead Clip Protocol**: If a Short receives < 500 views in 48 hours, analyze hook retention, re-cut the first 3 seconds, and repost as a fresh upload.
- **Velocity Rule**: Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes to boost initial engagement signals.

---

## 9. RETENTION TARGETS & AUDIT BENCHMARKS
- **70%+ Average View Duration (AVD)**: Algorithm expands distribution to tier-2 audience.
- **80%–90%+ with Re-watches**: Algorithmic viral zone.
- **< 50% in First 3 Seconds**: Hook failure. The first spoken line was too slow or frame 1 had low energy.
- **Mid-Clip Drop (Sec 8–15)**: Context buildup was too long; cut buildup faster.
- **End-Clip Drop**: Payoff was delayed or sluggish; execute a harder cut.

---

## 10. 2026 ALGORITHM PRIORITY SIGNALS
1. **Hook Speed (0–1.5s)**: First priority for feed swipe retention.
2. **Rewatch & Loop Flow**: Seamless end-to-beginning audio/video transition.
3. **First-Hour Engagement Velocity**: Likes, shares, and comment replies.
4. **Human Face Presence**: Emotionally charged facial expressions boost click-through and stop scroll.
5. **Sound-Off Caption Readability**: 70%+ of mobile users view with captions; karaoke yellow pops maximize watch time.

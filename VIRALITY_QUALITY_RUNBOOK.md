## 5. AUDIO ENGINEERING SPECIFICATION

*Architected from OpenCut-AI's multitrack audio timeline standards.*

### The 3-Track Multitrack Sonic Architecture

```
Track 1: [ DIALOGUE MASTER ] ── Normalized to -14.0 LUFS | High-Pass 80Hz | Peak Limiter -1.0 dBFS
Track 2: [ DYNAMIC BGM     ] ── Ducked to -14dB to -18dB during speech | Release 180ms
Track 3: [ IMPACT SFX      ] ── Peak aligned within ±40ms of word transient | 0dBFS relative
```

### Precise Sonic Parameters

1. **Dialogue Stem Processing**:
   - **Integrated Loudness**: $-14.0\text{ LUFS} \pm 0.5\text{ LUFS}$.
   - **True Peak**: $-1.0\text{ dBFS}$ max (prevents inter-sample distortion on AAC compression).
   - **EQ Profile**: High-pass filter at $85\text{Hz}$ (18dB/octave slope) to strip desk thumps; dynamic de-esser at $5.5\text{kHz} - 7.5\text{kHz}$ to suppress sibilance.

2. **Intelligent Background Music (BGM) Ducking**:
   - When vocal activity is detected: BGM gain ducks down by **$-14\text{dB}$ to $-18\text{dB}$** relative to dialogue.
   - **Attack Time**: $25\text{ms}$ (instant duck upon syllable onset).
   - **Hold Time**: $120\text{ms}$ (prevents ducking chatter between rapid syllables).
   - **Release Time**: $220\text{ms}$ (smooth musical swell when the speaker pauses).

3. **SFX Word-Transient Alignment**:
   - Impact sound effects (`pop.wav`, `whoosh.wav`, `vine_boom.wav`) must hit **within $\le 40\text{ms}$** of the primary vocal transient spike.
   - Desynchronization $> 75\text{ms}$ creates cognitive dissonance, lowering viewed retention by up to $14\%$.

4. **Deadpan Silence Drops**:
   - For comedic or "AYO / PAUSE" beats, background music must hard-cut to $-\infty\text{dB}$ for precisely $0.3\text{s} - 0.5\text{s}$.
   - The silence drop amplifies the awkward tension before the punchline or reaction sound effect lands.

## 7. TITLE & THUMBNAIL VERIFICATION FORMULA

A high-retention video with a poor title or thumbnail yields a low Click-Through Rate (CTR), stranding the video in the 0–100 view cohort.

### The Non-Duplicative Hook Pairing Law

The video title and thumbnail text must form a **complementary cognitive pair**. They must **NEVER** duplicate the same words.

```
Title:      "Speed's 1 Mistake That Broke GTA 6 Forever"  (Context / Curiosity)
                             ✕ NEVER DUPLICATE ✕
Thumb Text: "NO WAY BROKE IT! 😱"                         (Visceral Emotional Punch)
```

### Title Construction Rules

1. **Character Count**: Strictly **40 to 55 characters**. (Titles $\ge 56$ chars truncate on iOS and Android Shorts feeds).
2. **Mandatory Elements**:
   - Exact Proper Noun or Streamer Name (`Speed`, `Franklin`, `Trevor`, `Paji`).
   - Exactly One Specific Number or Measure (`1`, `3`, `$10,000`, `0.001s`).
   - High-Arousal Emotional Trigger (`Broke`, `Dumped`, `Exposed`, `Banned`, `Leaked`).
   - Exactly One High-Contrast Emoji at the end (`💀`, `🚨`, `💔`, `🔥`).

### Thumbnail Expression Extraction

1. **Facial Dynamics**: Use MediaPipe face detection to score frames by mouth open ratio, eyebrow raise, and eye aperture.
2. **Peak Audio Correlation**: Extract candidate thumbnail frames within $\pm 250\text{ms}$ of the highest acoustic amplitude spike (the scream, laugh, or gasp).
3. **Typography**: 3 to 4 words maximum in ALL CAPS, ultra-bold, angled at $-3^\circ$ with high-contrast colored stroke.

## 8. PYTHON-EXECUTABLE QUALITY GATE EVALUATION ENGINE

Below is the definitive, production-grade quality gate engine. This class will be imported and wired directly into `virality_engine.py` and `clipper.py` as the ultimate release gate.

```python
"""
Sovereign Master Virality Quality Gate v6.0
=========================================
Implements the 7-Stage Pre-Flight Verification Gate & Mathematical Scoring
for 2026 YouTube Shorts Algorithmic Domination.
"""

import re
import math
from typing import Dict, List, Any, Tuple


class MasterQualityGate:
    """
    Sovereign Quality Auditor.
    Computes strict mathematical scores and applies instant-fail filters.
    """
    MIN_PASSING_SCORE = 85.0  # Out of 100.0

    @classmethod
    def evaluate_clip(
        cls,
        clip_metadata: Dict[str, Any],
        transcript_words: List[Dict[str, Any]],
        audio_metrics: Dict[str, Any]
    ) -> Dict[str, Any]:
        """
        Executes full audit against the 7-Stage Gate and Emotional Tone Matrix.

        Args:
            clip_metadata: {
                "title": str,
                "thumbnail_text": str,
                "duration": float,
                "visual_changes_count": int,
                "badges_valid": bool,
                "has_fake_graphs": bool,
                "has_outro_fluff": bool
            }
            transcript_words: list of dicts [{"word": str, "start": float, "end": float, "confidence": float}]
            audio_metrics: {
                "dialogue_lufs": float,       # Ideal: -14.0
                "bgm_ducking_db": float,      # Ideal: -14.0 to -18.0
                "max_silence_gap": float,     # Ideal: <= 0.40s
                "first_word_latency": float,  # Ideal: <= 0.40s
                "sfx_transient_sync_ms": float# Ideal: <= 50.0ms
            }

        Returns:
            Dict containing detailed audit breakdown, scores, and PASS/FAIL verdict.
        """
        gate_failures: List[str] = []
        score_breakdown: Dict[str, float] = {}

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 1: FRAME 0 HOOK & LATENCY
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        first_word_lat = audio_metrics.get("first_word_latency", 99.0)
        if transcript_words and first_word_lat == 99.0:
            first_word_lat = transcript_words[0].get("start", 99.0)

        if first_word_lat > 0.45:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 1 Fail: First word latency too high ({first_word_lat:.2f}s > 0.40s threshold). Dead air at Frame 0.")
            score_breakdown["hook_velocity"] = 0.0
        else:
            hook_pts = max(0.0, 15.0 * (1.0 - (first_word_lat / 0.45)))
            score_breakdown["hook_velocity"] = round(hook_pts, 2)

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 2: ZERO DEAD SPACE (THE 400ms RULE)
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        max_silence = audio_metrics.get("max_silence_gap", 0.0)
        # Verify across transcript words if gaps exceed 0.40s
        calculated_max_gap = 0.0
        for i in range(len(transcript_words) - 1):
            gap = transcript_words[i+1]["start"] - transcript_words[i]["end"]
            if gap > calculated_max_gap:
                calculated_max_gap = gap
        
        effective_max_silence = max(max_silence, calculated_max_gap)
        if effective_max_silence > 0.42:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 2 Fail: Dead space detected ({effective_max_silence:.2f}s > 0.40s threshold). Violates speech rough-cut standard.")
            score_breakdown["dead_space"] = 0.0
        else:
            score_breakdown["dead_space"] = 15.0

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 3: MICRO-PACING RHYTHM (2.5s - 3.5s per visual event)
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        duration = clip_metadata.get("duration", 30.0)
        visual_events = clip_metadata.get("visual_changes_count", 0)
        expected_events = math.ceil(duration / 3.2)

        if duration < 20.0 or duration > 58.0:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 3 Fail: Clip duration ({duration:.1f}s) outside optimal 25-45s sweet spot.")
            score_breakdown["pacing_rhythm"] = 0.0
        elif visual_events < expected_events:
            pts = 15.0 * (visual_events / max(1, expected_events))
            score_breakdown["pacing_rhythm"] = round(pts, 2)
        else:
            score_breakdown["pacing_rhythm"] = 15.0

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 4: VISUAL ASSET VERACITY
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        if clip_metadata.get("has_fake_graphs", False):
            gate_failures.append("Gate 4 Fail: Fake / hallucinated tech graphs detected. Violates asset veracity policy.")
            score_breakdown["asset_veracity"] = 0.0
        elif not clip_metadata.get("badges_valid", True):
            gate_failures.append("Gate 4 Fail: Misaligned or invalid entity badges.")
            score_breakdown["asset_veracity"] = 5.0
        else:
            score_breakdown["asset_veracity"] = 15.0

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 5: KINETIC TYPOGRAPHY PRECISION
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # Check for transcription confidence and word clustering
        low_confidence_tokens = [w for w in transcript_words if w.get("confidence", 1.0) < 0.70]
        if len(low_confidence_tokens) > 3:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 5 Fail: High phonetic hallucination risk ({len(low_confidence_tokens)} low-confidence tokens).")
            score_breakdown["typography"] = 5.0
        else:
            score_breakdown["typography"] = 15.0

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 6: AUDIO ENGINEERING & DUCKING
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        bgm_duck = audio_metrics.get("bgm_ducking_db", -15.0)
        sfx_sync = audio_metrics.get("sfx_transient_sync_ms", 30.0)

        audio_pts = 15.0
        if bgm_duck > -12.0:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 6 Fail: BGM insufficiently ducked ({bgm_duck:.1f}dB > -14dB). Speech intelligibility compromised.")
            audio_pts -= 8.0
        if sfx_sync > 60.0:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 6 Fail: SFX transient desynchronized ({sfx_sync:.1f}ms > 50ms).")
            audio_pts -= 7.0

        score_breakdown["audio_engineering"] = max(0.0, audio_pts)

        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        # STAGE 7: TITLE, THUMBNAIL & LOOP INTEGRITY
        # -------------------------------------------------------------
        title = clip_metadata.get("title", "")
        thumb_text = clip_metadata.get("thumbnail_text", "")
        has_outro = clip_metadata.get("has_outro_fluff", False)

        title_pts = 10.0
        if has_outro:
            gate_failures.append("Gate 7 Fail: Outro fluff or subscription callout detected. Destroys infinite loop.")
            title_pts -= 5.0

        # Check title length: 40 - 55 chars
        if not (40 <= len(title) <= 55):
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 7 Fail: Title length ({len(title)} chars) outside 40-55 character limit.")
            title_pts -= 3.0

        # Check Non-Duplication Pairing
        title_words = set(re.sub(r"[^\w\s]", "", title.lower()).split())
        thumb_words = set(re.sub(r"[^\w\s]", "", thumb_text.lower()).split())
        overlap = title_words.intersection(thumb_words)
        if len(overlap) >= 2:
            gate_failures.append(f"Gate 7 Fail: Title and thumbnail text duplicate identical words ({overlap}).")
```
